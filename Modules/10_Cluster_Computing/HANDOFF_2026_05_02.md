## 1. Headline finding from today

**Cluster-scale freely-fit fails the same way compound and group_scale
did.** First-ever cluster_scale Cannon run (job 9675524, 1h wall) returned
χ²/N = 22.6, max\|res\| = 78σ, with `theta_E_star` (the FJ scaling
amplitude) railed to 0.10 (lower prior bound) and the BCG
einstein_radius ballooned to 5.95″ (truth 4.0″). The BCG-vs-FJ-amplitude
degeneracy collapsed in the wide-prior search.

This is the third architecture (after compound zoo Pattern A/E and
group_scale staged_satellites stall) where wide-prior freely-fit fails
on a multi-deflector lens system. The pattern is *consistent*: wide
priors + ≥2 mass components + degenerate sub-distance ratios → search
collapses to a non-physical basin.

**The truth-anchored protocol resolves it** for compound (group_scale
PASS in 1h05m, R5_truth_freecosmo in flight) and is now applied to
cluster_scale (job 9727867, in flight as of EOD).
