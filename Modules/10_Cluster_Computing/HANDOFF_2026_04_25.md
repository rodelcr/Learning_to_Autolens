## 2. Headline result — quad_time_delay P2 H0 recovery

Phase 1 (cosmology fixed at H0=70) recovered:
- θ_E = 1.243 ± 0.011 (truth 1.200, +3.6%)
- γ′ = 2.07 ± 0.078 (truth 2.0, +3.5%)
- ell_comps = (0.147, 0.186) (truth (0.10, 0.15)) — biased high by ~3-4σ

Phase 2 (H0 free, prior Uniform(40, 120)):
- H0 (max-likelihood) = **74.6 km/s/Mpc** (truth 70.0, +6.6%)
- H0 (posterior median) = **92.7 ± 9** (1σ: 82.6 - 105) — *skewed-right by MSD*
- log_evidence = -30.25, max log-likelihood = 15.55

The 18-unit gap between max-lik and posterior-median H0 is the
mass-sheet degeneracy in textbook form. The wide H0 prior + coupled γ′
mean Nautilus's posterior straddles a long ridge from the truth at
H0=70 out to ~110, with most volume above 90. **A single quad with
parametric mass and no external constraints cannot beat ~10% on H0.**
This is exactly why H0LiCOW/TDCOSMO need 6+ quads with stellar
kinematics constraints to break the MSD.

Phase 1 + 2 results pulled and committed at
`Examples/quad_time_delay/results/{phase_1_cosmology_fixed,phase_2_h0_free}/`.

## 3. New tooling shipped this cycle

### Lenstronomy → autolens convention reference

`Examples/mge_to_physical/mocks/PROVENANCE.md` codifies the cross-framework
parameter translation used by P3, P4, and now any future lenstronomy-mock
adoption:

- **Centre tuple axis swap.** Lenstronomy `(center_x, center_y)` → autolens
  `centre = (centre_0, centre_1)` where `centre_0` is y. Most subtle gotcha
  — forgetting it rotates the light centroid 90°.
- **SHEAR_GAMMA_PSI rotation.** `gamma_1 = γ_ext * cos(2 ψ_ext)`,
  `gamma_2 = γ_ext * sin(2 ψ_ext)`. Note the factor of 2 (shear is spin-2).
- **SERSIC_ELLIPSE e1, e2** pass through directly (same axis-ratio + PA flavour).
- **`amp` vs `intensity`** — same definition, just renamed.
- **Noise map** — built analytically from `truth.background_rms` and
  `truth.exp_time` per the `simulator.py` recipe. Lenstronomy mocks ship
  no noise FITS.

### Verified Bayes-factor reference table

`Examples/bayesian_model_comparison/README.md` has BibTeX-ready
references for Jeffreys 1961 / Kass & Raftery 1995 / Trotta 2008 / Suyu+
2006 / Vegetti & Koopmans 2009 / Vegetti+ 2010 / Lange 2023. All
cross-checked against arXiv / NASA ADS / journal sites. Two prior
misremembers corrected (Suyu 2006 is MNRAS 371 not ApJ 653; Lange 2023's
Nautilus log_Z accuracy on benchmarks is 0.01–0.03 not 0.1–1).

### slurm export-step bug fix

`submit_cannon.slurm`'s example-export step previously assumed every
driver wrote to `${OUTPUT_ROOT}/<example>/...`. Newer drivers (qtd,
mge, zoo, intf) write directly to `${OUTPUT_ROOT}/<unique_tag>/...`.
Slurm now falls back to scanning the whole `${OUTPUT_ROOT}` when the
example-subdir doesn't exist. Backward compatible with compound_lens /
disky_spiral / double_source_plane drivers that DO use the subdir.
