## 1. Headline finding from today

The R5_freecosmo rung (mocks 2, 3-null, 5) returned a clean **negative
result**: at our 9k-pixel multi-plane single-source-plane data quality,
the chain learns nothing about (Om0, w0) beyond the prior, and when the
lens model misfits, cosmology parameters absorb the misfit rather than
encode the truth.

| Mock | Truth (Om, w) | Recovered (Om, w) ±1σ | χ²/N | max\|res\| |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 (null) | (0.30, -1.0) | **(0.30 ±0.10, -1.00 ±0.20)** | 1.14 | 7.10σ |
| 2 | (0.25, **-0.9**) | (0.30 ±0.10, **-0.45 ±0.10**) | 1.75 | **15.1σ** |
| 5 | (0.35, **-1.2**) | (0.30 ±0.10, **-0.51 ±0.10**) | 1.23 | 7.9σ |

**The mock_3 1σ posterior widths are EXACTLY the prior σ widths**
(Om: ±0.10, w: ±0.20), proving the data added zero information.
**The mocks 2/5 medians moved toward w ≈ -0.5 in BOTH directions of
truth-mis-specification**, proving cosmology absorbed lens-model misfit
rather than recovered cosmology. (Mock_2's 15.1σ residual makes its
"cosmology" verdict completely unreliable; the chain found a
pathological non-physical basin.)

This is the *correct* pedagogical result and is documented in §15 of
`Examples/compound_lens_zoo/02_compound_lens_ladder.ipynb`. The
methodology now points toward β-search on DSPL / cluster_scale.
