## §4.3 O'Riordan BPL degeneracy — REPRODUCED (audited 2026-05-28)

Compared the tied-centre (coaxial) BPL pair (`bpl_3sersic_v2`,
`bpl_smbh_3sersic_v2`) against the canonical PL ladder. ΔlnZ ladder:

| Model | logZ | ΔlnZ |
|---|---|---|
| PL no-SMBH (v4) | 174904.39 | — |
| PL + SMBH | 174925.68 | **+21.29** (the PL "detection") |
| BPL tied no-SMBH | 174916.23 | **+11.84 over PL** |
| BPL tied + SMBH | 174917.96 | **+1.73 over BPL** |

**Result:** a BPL with NO SMBH recovers +11.84 of the +21.29 the SMBH gave in
the PL model → **~56% of the "SMBH signal" is degenerate with inner-mass-slope
freedom**. Once the BPL is allowed, the SMBH adds only +1.73. This is exactly the
O'Riordan/N+23 §4.3 degeneracy: the broken profile mimics the SMBH's central
concentration by **railing `break_radius`→0.056″ (0.05 floor) + steepening
`inner_slope`→1.44**. The tied-centre BPL recovers the **canonical θ_E=1.957″**
(✓, unlike the FAILed free-centre run), SMBH undetected (θ_E≈0.0067″).

**Verdict: SUSPECT, not a clean reproduction** — `break_radius` AND `outer_slope`
both rail at their floors, and the 1σ residual still shows unsubtracted cluster
members. So we cannot yet claim the SMBH *definitively* vanishes under BPL; our
BPL baseline is itself incompletely converged. **Same fix as every A1201 variant:
pixelised adapt source + member subtraction** (the `15715540`/`15717750`/`15717768`
adapt fits running now). Audit artifacts: `_audit_2026_05_28/bpl_tied_{nosmbh,smbh}/`.
