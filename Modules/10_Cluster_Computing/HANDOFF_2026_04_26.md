### Debt 2: P3 +288 Bayes factor caveat added
Opening Search 3's `fit_subplot.png` revealed ~10σ coherent ring
residuals — both stars-only (Search 2) and stars+DM (Search 3) are
gross misspecifications relative to truth (omit secondary deflector at
z=0.8, omit second source). The Δlog_Z=+288 favouring stars+DM is the
text-book P5 §5.4 *model misspecification* case: between two wrong
models, the Bayes factor picks the less-wrong one but doesn't validate
either. **Updates**: P3 README §Caveats with 3 concrete fixes (add 2nd
source, add secondary deflector, tighten lens-light mask); P5 +288 row
tagged ⚠️ with pointer to §5.4; LEARNING_LOG entry on
"numbers-without-pictures applies to Bayes factors too."
