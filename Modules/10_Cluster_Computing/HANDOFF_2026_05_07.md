### Curriculum expansion — **Module 12 (TDCOSMO + MSD) is the priority**
The curriculum table in `CLAUDE.md` lists Module 12 as "Time-Delay Cosmography & MSD" but the notebook does not exist. **Modules 13 (kinematics) and 14 (multi-plane) DO exist** — Module 13 starts with "presumes Mod 12 derived the basic TD-relation" so the gap shows.

**Module 12 outline** (~25 cells):

1. *Setup* — what time delays are in lensing. Refsdal 1964. The four images of a quad arrive at different observed times; the difference Δt = (1+z_l) τ × Δϕ where τ = D_Δt/c is the time-delay distance and Δϕ is the Fermat potential difference at conjugate image positions.
2. *Fermat potential derivation* — geometric path delay + Shapiro (gravitational) delay → Fermat potential ϕ(θ). Fermat's principle. Cite S92 Ch.5.
3. *D_Δt as a cosmography observable* — D_Δt depends on H0 mostly, weakly on Om/w. Refsdal's original 1964 idea + first measurement (Schechter+97 on PG1115).
4. *Mass-sheet degeneracy (MSD)* — derivation. The transformation κ → λκ + (1−λ) preserves all *observables* except Δt and σ_v. Show the math: image positions, magnification ratios, time delays scale as λ; H0 inferred from Δt scales as 1/λ.
5. *TDCOSMO chain (no kinematics yet)* — Wong+20 (H0LiCOW), Birrer+20 (TDCOSMO I-III). The pipeline: image modeling → external convergence → time delay → H0. Each link's systematic budget.
6. *Worked example with `Examples/quad_time_delay/`* — currently scaffolded only. Module 12 ships the *theory + a hand-computed time-delay example*; the audited Cannon fit is a v0.95 follow-up under `Examples/quad_time_delay/`.
7. *Why Module 13 exists* — MSD is the dominant systematic. Kinematics breaks it (next module).

Files: `Modules/12_Time_Delay_Cosmography/12_time_delay_cosmography.ipynb`, `Solutions/12_time_delay_cosmography_SOLVED.ipynb`. Notebook is pedagogical (no Cannon dependency) so it ships fast.
