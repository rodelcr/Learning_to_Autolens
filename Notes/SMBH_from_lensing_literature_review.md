## I. Radial arcs and the inner density slope (1987--2008)

The foundational insight: radial arcs form near the radial critical curve, whose position is exquisitely sensitive to the inner mass profile slope. This is the theoretical root of SMBH-from-lensing.

| Year | Authors | Journal | arXiv / Ref | Key contribution |
|------|---------|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| 1987 | Soucail, Fort, Mellier & Picat | A&A 172, L14 | pre-arXiv | First spectroscopic confirmation of a gravitational arc (A370). Established arcs as real astrophysical phenomena. |
| 1991 | Miralda-Escude | ApJ 370, 1 | pre-arXiv; ADS `1991ApJ...370....1M` | Formalism for inverting observed arcs into cluster mass maps. Framework later used to connect radial/tangential arc positions to radial density profile. |
| **1992** | **Fort, Le Fevre, Hammer & Cailloux** | **ApJ 399, L125** | pre-arXiv; ADS `1992ApJ...399L.125F` | **First detection of a radial arc** (MS 2137-23). Radial image embedded within cD galaxy halo implies small core radius. First to note radial+tangential arc geometry uniquely constrains the inner mass profile. Launched the radial-arc-as-inner-slope-probe programme. |
| 1993 | Mellier, Fort & Kneib | ApJ 407, 33 | pre-arXiv; ADS `1993ApJ...407...33M` | Modeled MS 2137 (tangential + radial arcs + arclets). Showed radial arc position tightly constrains core radius and inner slope. |
| 1994 | Bartelmann & Weiss | A&A 287, 1 | `astro-ph/9311074` | Ray-tracing cross-section method for computing tangential + radial arc statistics from numerically simulated clusters. |
| 1994 | Fort & Mellier | A&A Rev. 5, 239 | pre-arXiv | Comprehensive review of giant arcs and arclets, including MS 2137 radial arc implications for cluster core structure. |
| **1995** | **Miralda-Escude** | **ApJ 438, 514** | pre-arXiv; ADS `1995ApJ...438..514M` | **Key theory paper.** Ratio of radial arc distance to counter-image distance constrains the form of the density profile. Applied to MS 2137. Most direct early connection of radial arc position to inner slope. |
| **1996** | **Bartelmann** | **A&A 313, 697** | `astro-ph/9602053` | Showed NFW profiles (central cusp rho ~ r^{-1}) **always possess a radial critical curve** and can produce radial arcs -- counter to the then-prevailing argument that radial arcs require a flat core. Both NFW parameters determinable from radial+tangential arcs. |
| 1996 | Narayan & Bartelmann | Saas-Fee lectures | `astro-ph/9606001` | Standard pedagogical reference for lambda_r, lambda_t eigenvalues, radial/tangential critical curves, and their connection to convergence gradient. |
| 1997 | Hammer, Teyssandier, Shaya+ | ApJL | `astro-ph/9610207` | Deep HST imaging of MS 2137 resolving detailed morphology of radial arc system. |
| **2001** | **Molikawa & Hattori** | ApJL (submitted) | `astro-ph/0009343` | Radial-to-tangential arc number ratio depends strongly on central slope, weakly on other cluster parameters. Proposed radial arc statistics as a clean single-parameter probe of the inner slope. |
| 2001 | Oguri, Taruya & Suto | ApJ 559, 572 | `astro-ph/0105248` | Joint tangential+radial arc ratio as discriminator between cuspy (NFW) and cored profiles. |
| **2002** | **Sand, Treu & Ellis** | **ApJ 574, L129** | `astro-ph/0207048` | **First direct measurement of inner DM slope from radial arcs.** MS 2137: beta < 0.9 at 99% CL -- significantly shallower than NFW (beta=1). Sparked the cluster cusp/core controversy. |
| 2003 | Gavazzi, Fort, Mellier+ | A&A 403, 11 | `astro-ph/0212214` | Independent VLT reanalysis of MS 2137, checking Sand+02 slope result. |
| **2004** | **Sand, Treu, Smith & Ellis** | **ApJ 604, 88** | `astro-ph/0309465` | **Extended to 6 clusters.** Three with radial+tangential arcs provide strong constraints. Mean inner DM slope beta = 0.52 +/- 0.05 -- significantly shallower than NFW. Most influential paper in the cluster cusp/core debate. |
| **2008** | **Sand, Treu, Ellis, Smith & Kneib** | **ApJ 674, 711** | `0710.1069` | Full 2D lensing + BCG stellar dynamics for A383 and MS 2137. Joint constraints separate baryonic vs DM, confirming inner DM slope shallower than NFW once BCG stellar mass is subtracted. **Methodology precursor to Nightingale+23 and Melo-Carneiro+25.** |

## II. Central images, odd-image theorem, and SMBH constraints (2001--2016)

Parallel thread: the odd-image theorem guarantees a faint central image -- its brightness (or absence) constrains the central density and therefore the SMBH mass.

| Year | Authors | Journal | arXiv / Ref | Key contribution |
|------|---------|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| **2001** | **Mao, Witt & Koopmans** | **MNRAS 323, 301** | `astro-ph/0007011` | **First systematic model of SMBH effects on lens critical curves.** Isothermal ellipsoid + core + central BH. Above a critical M_BH, central images completely suppressed. Below it, a "zone of influence" exists. The theoretical bridge from radial critical curves to SMBH detection. |
| **2001** | **Rusin & Ma** | **ApJ 549, L33** | `astro-ph/0009079` | Flipped the argument: *absence* of odd images in 6 radio lenses -> inner slope beta > 0.80 (90% CL). Non-detection as constraint on central density. |
| 2002 | Evans & Hunter | ApJ 575, 68 | `astro-ph/0204206` | Magnification properties of cored galaxy models; central image demagnification depends on core size + slope. Analytical framework. |
| **2003** | **Keeton** | **ApJ 582, 17** | `astro-ph/0206243` | Quantitative framework: predicted central image magnifications span 6 orders of magnitude across realistic galaxy models. Established why central images are so hard to detect, and what sensitivity is needed. |
| 2003 | Winn, Rusin & Kochanek | ApJ 587, 80 | `astro-ph/0212423` | Multi-frequency VLBA of PMN J1632-0033, investigating candidate central image. Precursor to the Nature paper. |
| **2004** | **Winn, Rusin & Kochanek** | **Nature 427, 613** | `astro-ph/0312136` | **First secure detection of a central (odd) image** in a gravitational lens (PMN J1632-0033, radio). Constrains M_BH < 2x10^8 M_sun. Proof of concept that central images exist and can probe BH mass. |
| 2004 | Bowman, Hewitt & Kiger | ApJ 617, 81 | `astro-ph/0406568` | SMBH either blocks core image or creates extra demagnified image in cored-isothermal + broken-power-law models. Flux ratio ~10^6 needed for detection -- requires next-gen radio. |
| **2005** | **Rusin, Keeton & Winn** | **ApJ 627, L93** | `astro-ph/0412034` | When central image *pair* is detected (SMBH can split central image), M_BH measurable to <0.1 dex. Even with near-complete ignorance of inner mass, M_BH constrainable to factor ~10. The quantitative roadmap for SMBH measurement. |
| 2005 | Inada+ | PASJ 57, L7 | `astro-ph/0503310` | Fifth (central) image of cluster-lensed quasar SDSS J1004+4112 via HST. First central image in cluster-scale lens. |
| 2006 | Boyce, Winn, Hewitt & Myers | ApJ 648, 73 | `astro-ph/0605178` | ELVIS survey: non-detection in PMN J1838-3427 -> gamma > 1.93. Null results from central image searches provide tight inner-profile constraints. |
| 2007 | Dobler, Keeton & Wambsganss | MNRAS 377, 977 | `astro-ph/0507522` | Central images more susceptible to microlensing than other images -- probe stars vs DM distribution in galaxy cores. |
| 2007 | Zhang, Jackson, Porcas & Browne | MNRAS 377, 1623 | `astro-ph/0703226` | B1030+074 central image upper limit (180 uJy); requires steep cusp or massive SMBH. |
| 2008 | Inada+ | PASJ 60, L27 | `0808.1769` | Spectroscopic confirmation of SDSS J1004 central image. M_BH < 2.1x10^10 M_sun at z=0.68, consistent with local M_BH-sigma_*. First SMBH constraint from spectroscopically confirmed central image. |
| 2015 | Hezaveh, Marshall & Blandford | ApJL 799, L22 | `1501.01757` | Deep ALMA should enable central image detection at 3-sigma across broad parameter range. Fisher analysis: M_BH + core size constrainable. Key paper motivating ALMA central-image searches. |
| 2015 | Wong, Suyu & Matsushita | ApJ 811, 115 | `1503.05558` | ALMA observations of SDP.81: non-detection -> log(M_BH/M_sun) > 8.5. Directly demonstrates the slope-vs-BH-mass tradeoff from sub-mm data. |
| **2016** | **Quinn+** | **MNRAS 459, 2394** | `1604.00173` | Deepest central-image limit in B1030+074 (VLA 22 GHz + eMERLIN 5 GHz). Non-detection requires SMBH influence OR gamma ~ isothermal. ~45% of comparable ellipticals should produce detectable central images. |

## III. The Abell 1201 sequence and modern SMBH detections (2017--2025)

The two threads converge: radial arc morphology + kinematics replaces central image detection as the SMBH probe.

| Year | Authors | Journal | arXiv / Ref | Key contribution |
|------|---------|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| **2017** | **Smith, Lucey & Edge** | **MNRAS 467, 836** | `1701.02745` | **Discovery of counter-image at 0.6" from A1201 BCG.** Models require *either* M_BH ~ 1.3x10^10 M_sun *or* Kroupa->Salpeter IMF gradient. **The gamma'-M_BH degeneracy paper for A1201.** Direct precursor to N+23. |
| **2017** | **Smith** | **MNRAS 471, 383** | `1706.07055` | IFU kinematics of A1201 BCG with axisymmetric Jeans models. Lensing mass anchoring breaks M/L-DM degeneracy: DM inner slope = 1.0 +/- 0.1 (NFW-consistent), M_cen ~ 2.5x10^10 M_sun. Sets stage for N+23's full model. |
| 2018 | Chen, Broadhurst+ | ApJ 863, 135 | `1805.05051` | SMBH in MACS J1149 BCG: (8.4 +4.3/-1.8) x 10^9 M_sun from arc curvature near BCG centre. Offset 4.4 kpc from BCG -- merger kick. |
| **2023** | **Mahler, Natarajan, Jauzac & Richard** | **MNRAS 518, 54** | `2201.10900` | Theory: SMBHs in clusters split or erase the radial critical curve. Radial caustic area 0.4 arcsec^2 (10^9 M_sun) to 5 arcsec^2 (10^10 M_sun). Formalises the radial-arc-SMBH connection at cluster scale. |
| **2023** | **Nightingale, Smith, He+ (12 authors)** | **MNRAS 521, 3298** | `2303.15514` | **First SMBH mass measurement from lensing alone, without a central image.** A1201 BCG: M_BH = (3.27 +/- 2.12) x 10^10 M_sun (3-sigma lower limit ~10^10). Bayesian model comparison decisively favours SMBH. Lensed arc passes ~1 kpc from BCG centre near radial critical curve. PyAutoLens + multi-band HST. |
| 2023 | Perera, Williams & Scarlata | MNRAS 522, 1863 | `2304.03795` | Offset SMBH distorts time-delay surface -> 3 central images (near-BH + max + saddle). Uses AGN offset distribution to predict central image observability. |
| **2025** | **Melo-Carneiro, Collett, Oldham+** | **MNRAS 541, 2853** | `2502.13788` | **Second SMBH detection via lensing: Cosmic Horseshoe.** Joint MUSE IFU kinematics + HST lensing. log10(M_BH/M_sun) = 10.56 +0.07/-0.08 +/- 0.12(sys) -- **5-sigma, ~36 x 10^9 M_sun.** Radial arc near lens centre is the key constraint. Compound geometry (2 source redshifts) breaks MSD. ~1.5-sigma above M_BH-sigma_e relation. |
| **2025** | **Cerny, Jauzac, Lagattuta+** | **MNRAS 541, 2341** | `2506.21531` | **Kaleidoscope survey:** systematic catalogue of 6 galaxy clusters with radial arcs. Lensing + kinematics -> mean DM inner slope ~0.66 (sub-NFW). These are the future SMBH targets. |

## V. The narrative arc

**1990s -- The question.** Fort+1992 discovers the first radial arc. Miralda-Escude (1995) and Bartelmann (1996) show radial arc position constrains the inner mass slope. Sand+2002/2004/2008 measure inner DM slopes shallower than NFW in clusters using radial arcs + kinematics.

**2000s -- The bridge.** Mao+2001 shows SMBHs modify critical curves and suppress central images. Keeton (2003) quantifies the demagnification. Winn+2004 detects the first central image. Rusin+2005 shows central image pairs can weigh BHs to 0.1 dex. A decade of radio campaigns (Boyce+06, Zhang+07, Quinn+16) pushes sensitivity but finds few detections -- central images are too faint for current instruments.

**2017 -- The pivot.** Smith+2017 (x2) discovers a counter-image in A1201 and identifies the gamma'-M_BH degeneracy: the lensed morphology near the radial critical curve constrains the SMBH mass *without* needing a central image. This shifts the method from "detect the faint central image" to "model the radial arc geometry."

**2023 -- The detection.** Nightingale+2023 makes the first SMBH mass measurement from lensing alone (A1201, M_BH ~ 3x10^10 M_sun), using the arc morphology ~1 kpc from the BCG, near the radial critical curve. Key insight: with sufficiently high-resolution imaging + Bayesian model comparison, the radial arc shape is sensitive enough to detect a UMBH without ever seeing the central image.

**2025 -- Confirmation + kinematics.** Melo-Carneiro+2025 achieves a 5-sigma detection in the Cosmic Horseshoe (~36x10^9 M_sun) by jointly fitting a radial arc + spatially resolved IFU kinematics. This is the methodology operationalised in Phase 3 (`_jeans_sigma_v.py`): kinematics breaks the gamma'-M_BH degeneracy that imaging alone cannot resolve.

**Next -- AGEL spiral-lens programme.** Shajib et al. and Ferrami et al. (in prep) apply this to Einstein spirals at z~0.7, where the spiral source morphology provides additional constraints. Module 15 + `Examples/radial_arc_smbh/` teaches this theory; the A1201 paper-reproduction programme (Spec 05, private/) validates the N+23 methodology end-to-end.

## VI bis. The BPL ↔ SMBH degeneracy in our paper-repro programme (2026-05-27)

The Smith+2017 γ′-M_BH degeneracy materialises concretely in our A1201
reproduction (Spec 05) and the AGEL DESJ0206 work in a way that's worth
recording: a broken-power-law (BPL) mass model can fully *absorb* a
genuine central SMBH signal, masking the detection. The key diagnostic
is the BPL `break_radius` posterior position relative to the prior bounds.

### DESJ0206 (Ferrami et al. in prep)

When the AGEL collaboration fit DESJ0206 with `al.mp.PowerLawBroken`, the
posterior converged to **r_break ≤ 10 pc** (~0.005″ at z=0.45). At this
scale the BPL is operationally identical to a PL + central point-mass — the
"break" is so tight that ρ(r) ∝ r^{−γ_in} ⊕ r^{−γ_out} effectively reduces
to ρ(r) ∝ r^{−γ_out} + δ(r) · M_BH. **The BPL had become a disguised SMBH.**
This is what motivates the §3.4-style "BPL alternative" model in N+23 §3.4
serving as a model-comparison null for the PL+SMBH hypothesis.

### A1201 (Nightingale+2023, our Spec 05 reproduction)

Reproducing the N+23 BPL alternative with prior
`break_radius ~ U(0.05, 3.0)″` (the autolens default) and 3-Sersic light
(`bpl_3sersic_v2`, Cannon job 15698816, completed 2026-05-26), the
posterior is:

| Parameter         | Posterior median (1σ)          | Prior bounds      | Rail-pinned? |
|------------------|---------------------------------|--------------------|--------------|
| break_radius      | 0.056″ (0.052–0.065″) ≈ 160 pc | U(0.05, 3.0)       | **yes** at lower bound |
| inner_slope       | 1.44 (1.37–1.49)                | U(0.5, 2.5)        | no            |
| outer_slope       | 0.51 (0.50–0.52)                | U(0.5, 2.5)        | **yes** at lower bound |
| ΔlnZ(+SMBH)       | +1.73 (inconclusive)            | LogU(1e-3, 0.5) on θ_E_BH | — |

The same posterior position holds for the `bpl_smbh_3sersic_v2` (Cannon
15698828) — adding an explicit SMBH on top of the BPL leaves the BPL break
parameters unchanged and gives `smbh.einstein_radius = 0.007″ (0.001–0.120)`
— a posterior consistent with zero. The BPL is already absorbing the SMBH
effect by holding `break_radius` at the prior floor.

To definitively test this, we queued Cannon job **16341076** (2026-05-27)
with `EXTENDED_BREAK_PRIOR=1`, which switches the break_radius prior to
`LogUniform(1e-3, 3.0)″` — allowing r_break to collapse to ≈3 pc if the data
prefer it. Expected outcomes:

- **r_break → ~3 pc** → confirms BPL is a disguised SMBH at A1201, exactly
  as in DESJ0206. The BPL's previous lnZ deficit vs PL+SMBH is "explained
  by" the BPL being a noisier parametrisation of the same underlying mass
  structure (point mass at lens centre).
- **r_break stays ~50 mas with floor removed** → there's a real NSC-scale
  feature at 160 pc that the prior wasn't blocking.

### Why this matters for the methodology

This phenomenon makes Smith+2017's framing precise: **"the radial arc
constrains the inner mass slope"** is itself sensitive to *what
parametrisation you give the inner mass freedom to*. If you allow a free
break in the power-law, the data can absorb point-mass signal into break
position. If you forbid that freedom (PL + SMBH model), the same data
become "decisive" for an M_BH.

This is why the N+23 §3.9 evidence ladder includes BPL as a separate row,
and why Bayesian model comparison across mass-class alternatives — not
within-class ΔlnZ alone — is the right statistic. We codify this lesson
in our memory as
`feedback_smbh_detection_requires_converged_baseline.md`: SMBH "detection"
of ΔlnZ +O(20) is only meaningful relative to a baseline that has
NO competing inner-mass freedom (smooth PL, no BPL break, fully converged
light + source models).
